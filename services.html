Review Engine

★ Marketing trio $600 setup · $79/mo 3-day delivery

When a job is marked complete, AI sends a personalized review request, follows up after 3 days if ignored, and alerts you to negative reviews before they go public.

Every service business depends on reviews — and most owners forget to ask. We install an automation tied to your job-completion event (in your CRM, scheduling tool, or a manual trigger) that sends a personalized review request with the customer's name and the job done, follows up if they ignore it, and pings you the moment a negative one comes in so you can respond first.

What it does

  • Trigger on job complete (from your CRM, calendar, or a button)
  • Personalized SMS or email review request
  • Follow-up after 72 hours if no review left
  • Sentiment detection — flags negative reviews to you before they post
  • Funnel for unhappy customers to email you privately first
  • Weekly summary of new reviews and average rating trend

Best for

  • HVAC, plumbing, dentists, roofers — service businesses living on reviews
  • Any business with at least 5 jobs/week
  • Operators who know they should ask for reviews and never remember
