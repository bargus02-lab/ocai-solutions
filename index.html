06 — Common questions

Things you're probably wondering.

01 Do I need an existing CRM or tool stack?
Helpful but not required. If you have HubSpot, Pipedrive, Google Sheets, QuickBooks, or a similar tool, we build into it. If you don't, we'll set up something free (Google Sheets, a free CRM tier) as part of the setup.
02 Why a monthly retainer?
Automations break. APIs change, your tools update, your business shifts. The retainer covers monitoring, edge-case fixes, prompt tuning when the AI's responses drift, and small adjustments you'll want over time. If you want a one-time install with no retainer, we can do that — just for 2× the setup fee.
03 What's the Custom AI Workflow service?
When the three productized services don't fit, we build something custom. Common requests: inbox triage, scheduling automation, intake form to proposal, document extraction, AI receptionist, internal knowledge bot. Starts at $2,500 setup + $400/mo. Same fixed-scope, fixed-timeline format.
04 Do you only serve Orange County businesses?
No — the work is fully remote and we've shipped to clients across the U.S. The "Orange County" framing is about where we're based and the kind of operator-led service you'll get, not who we'll serve.
05 Can I bundle multiple services?
Yes — and we recommend it. Stack all three of the productized trio (Lead Response + Quote Widget + Review Engine) at setup and your combined price is $1,650 setup + $257/mo (vs $1,950 buying separately — $300 savings). Retainers stay separate so you can cancel any single service without losing the rest.

Email ocaisolution@gmail.com or use the contact form. We reply within one business day to schedule a 20-min discovery call. After that you get a written proposal with fixed scope and timeline.
