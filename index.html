05 Common questions Everything most people ask before they buy.

Things you're probably wondering.

01 Do I need an existing CRM or tool stack?
Helpful but not required. If you have HubSpot, Pipedrive, Google Sheets, QuickBooks, or a similar tool, we build into it. If you don't, we'll set up something free (Google Sheets, a free CRM tier) as part of the setup.
02 Why a monthly retainer?
Automations break. APIs change, your tools update, your business shifts. The retainer covers monitoring, edge-case fixes, prompt tuning when the AI's responses drift, and small adjustments you'll want over time. If you want a one-time install with no retainer, we can do that — just for 2× the setup fee.
03 Can I cancel the retainer?
Yes. Month-to-month. Cancel anytime via email, no penalties. The automation keeps running in your tools after you cancel — you just won't have us actively monitoring or tuning it. If something breaks later, we offer hourly fixes.
04 Do you serve businesses outside Orange County?
Yes. The work is fully remote — we've worked with businesses across the U.S. The "Orange County" framing is about where we're based, not who we serve.
05 Can I bundle multiple services?
Yes — and we recommend it. Stack all three at setup and your combined setup price is $1,650 (vs $1,950 buying separately — a $300 savings). Monthly retainers total $257/mo across the three and stay separate, so you can cancel any single service without losing the rest.
