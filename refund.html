Refund Policy.

Last updated:

OCAi Solutions services have two parts — a one-time setup fee and a monthly retainer. Each has its own refund and cancellation rules. The summary: setup is refundable before we begin, monthly retainers cancel anytime but aren't pro-rated for the month already paid.

1. Setup Fees

Setup fees are charged before installation begins. They cover the operator time required to scope, build, test, and hand off your automation.

Before installation begins

  • You can cancel and receive a 100% refund of the setup fee any time before we start technical work.
  • "Technical work" means the operator has begun installing the automation in your accounts — typically within 1–2 business days of the kickoff call.
  • Email ocaisolution@gmail.com with "refund setup" in the subject. Refund processed within five business days.

After installation begins

  • Setup fees are non-refundable once installation work has started, because operator time has already been spent.
  • If we cannot deliver what was promised in your written proposal, we refund the unearned portion of the setup fee in good faith.
  • If the automation as built doesn't match the proposal, we fix it free of charge.

2. Monthly Retainers

Monthly retainers cover ongoing monitoring, edge-case fixes, prompt tuning, and small adjustments. They're billed monthly in advance starting from the date of automation handoff.

Cancelling the retainer

  • Cancel any time by email — no penalties, no annual contracts.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month. The month already paid is not refunded.
  • Email ocaisolution@gmail.com with "cancel retainer" in the subject; you'll get a confirmation within one business day.

What happens to the automation after cancellation

  • The automation continues running in your tools (your Zapier, Make, n8n, Gmail, CRM, etc.). It's yours; you own it.
  • You lose ongoing monitoring, tuning, and small adjustments.
  • If something breaks later, we offer one-off hourly fixes at $150/hour, or you can re-engage the retainer at the regular monthly rate.

3. Disputes and Chargebacks

Before filing a chargeback with your bank, please email us. In nearly every case we'd rather work out a refund or fix on the spot than fight about it. Chargebacks cost us extra processing fees and waste both of our time.

If you file a chargeback without contacting us first and we have already delivered the automation, we may dispute the chargeback with the proposal, runbook, and delivery records as documentation.

4. Exceptions

Refunds and cancellation rights are not available where:

  • The customer has materially misrepresented their business, scope, or technical setup at the time of order.
  • The customer has used our deliverables in violation of our Terms of Service.
  • The customer has previously cancelled and re-engaged in a pattern that suggests bad-faith use of the cancellation policy.

5. Contact

Refund and cancellation requests, plus any questions: ocaisolution@gmail.com. We respond within one business day, usually faster.
