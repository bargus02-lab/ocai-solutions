About OCAi Solutions

A solo Orange County operator using AI to ship the work most businesses can't get to.

OCAi Solutions is run by Gus, a solo operator based in Orange County, California. The company was founded on a simple idea: most businesses know their inbound leads, quoting process, review collection, or sales reporting are eating their week — but the path from "we should automate this" to "it's running on its own" is too long, too expensive, or too vague. By the time the agency comes back with a proposal, the busy season is over.

Modern AI changes the math. An automation that used to take an agency three weeks of strategy decks and engineering can be installed in a week using tools you already pay for. The same shift applies to lead response, instant quoting, review collection, custom workflow builds, and self-refreshing sales dashboards.

OCAi Solutions packages that shift into five productized services: Instant Lead Response, Instant Quote Widget, Review Engine, Custom AI Workflow builds, and Sales Report Rescue. Lower-ticket services start at $600 setup. Bigger custom builds start at $2,500. Every engagement ends with a running system you own, not slides describing one.
