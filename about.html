About OCAI Solutions

A solo Orange County operator using AI to ship the work most small businesses can't get to.

OCAI Solutions is run by Gus, a solo operator based in Orange County, California. The company was founded on a simple idea: most local service businesses know their inbox, voicemail, and back-office are eating their week — but the path from "we should automate this" to "it's running on its own" is too long, too expensive, or too vague. By the time the agency comes back with a proposal, the busy season is over.

Modern AI changes the math. An automation that used to take an agency three weeks of strategy decks and engineering can be installed in a week using tools you already pay for. The same shift applies to lead response, voicemail triage, proposals, reviews, support inbox, and back-office bookkeeping.

OCAI Solutions exists to package that shift into six things small businesses can actually buy. Our flagship Instant Lead Response is a flat $750 setup + $99/mo. Bigger automations have higher setup fees but the same shape — fixed scope, fixed timeline. Every engagement ends with a running system you own, not slides describing one.
