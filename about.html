About OCAI Solutions

A solo Orange County operator using AI to ship the work most small businesses can't get to.

OCAI Solutions is run by Gus, a solo operator based in Santa Ana, California. The company was founded on a simple idea: most local service businesses know their website is bad — but the path from "we should fix this" to "it's actually fixed" is too long, too expensive, or too slow. By the time the agency calls back, the busy season is over.

Modern AI changes the math. A homepage that used to take an agency three weeks of strategy decks and discovery calls can be rewritten by hand in a few hours, sharpened by AI, and shipped tomorrow. The same shift applies to lead generation, follow-ups, and the slow-burn busywork that eats every operator's week.

OCAI Solutions exists to package that shift into things small businesses can actually buy. The Website Rescue is a flat $197. The bigger projects are quoted up front. Every engagement ends with deliverables you can use, not slides describing them.
