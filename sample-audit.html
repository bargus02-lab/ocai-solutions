Top 5 Conversion Killers — Punch List

1. Phone number is buried and not tap-to-call on mobile

Why it costs money: Most HVAC searches happen on a phone during an emergency, and visitors leave within seconds if they can't tap to call.

The fix: Pin the phone number as a tap-to-call link in the top right of every page header and at the end of every section.

2. Broken email address in the footer

Why it costs money: Customers who try to email get a bounce or just leave, and it signals the site is sloppy and untrustworthy.

The fix: Replace with a real monitored inbox like service@yourdomain.com today.

3. Weak, generic CTAs ("Call Now," "Learn More")

Why it costs money: Generic buttons don't tell visitors what they get, so fewer people click.

The fix: Change every CTA to a specific outcome like "Get My Free Quote," "Book Same-Day Service," or "Call (XXX) XXX-XXXX."

4. No pricing anchor or upfront offer

Why it costs money: Visitors comparing 3 contractors pick the one who shows a number, and you lose the click to the competitor with a "$89 service call" headline.

The fix: Add a visible offer to the hero — e.g. "$89 diagnostic" or "Free estimates on installs," plus a veteran/military/first responder discount badge.

5. No reviews count, star rating, or trust badges above the fold

Why it costs money: Homeowners letting strangers into their house need proof of trust before they call, and 5 unverified text quotes lower on the page don't do it.

The fix: Add a Google rating block (4.8 stars from 24 reviews) plus license number, "Insured," "Family-Owned," and "Since 1994" badges directly under the hero headline.
